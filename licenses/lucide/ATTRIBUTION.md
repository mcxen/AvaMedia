# Lucide viewer toolbar icons

Selected SVGs from `lucide-static` 1.55.0: https://lucide.dev/icons/

Upstream: https://github.com/lucide-icons/lucide

License: ISC, with the upstream MIT notice for Feather-derived icons. The unmodified upstream license is in `LICENSE` beside this file.

The viewer bundles 23 SVGs locally and renders their original geometry with theme-aware strokes. It does not contact Iconify or Lucide at runtime. The selected names, package archive integrity and per-file hashes are recorded in `src/AvaMedia.Desktop/Assets/ToolbarIcons/Lucide/sources.json`.
