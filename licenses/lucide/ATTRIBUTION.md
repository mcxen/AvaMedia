# Lucide toolbar and action icons

Selected SVGs from `lucide-static` 1.55.0: https://lucide.dev/icons/

Upstream: https://github.com/lucide-icons/lucide

License: ISC, with the upstream MIT notice for Feather-derived icons. The unmodified upstream license is in `LICENSE` beside this file.

The application bundles SVGs locally for the image viewer, common action buttons, media transports and WiFi transfer page, and renders their original geometry with theme-aware strokes. It does not contact Iconify or Lucide at runtime. The selected names, package archive integrity and per-file hashes are recorded in `src/AvaMedia.Desktop/Assets/ToolbarIcons/Lucide/sources.json`. Exact, reviewed action-label mappings are recorded in `src/AvaMedia.Desktop/Assets/ToolbarIcons/ActionButtonIcons.json`; ambiguous functions keep their text labels.
